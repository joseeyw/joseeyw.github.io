About Me

I'm a Senior Software Engineer based in Nairobi, Kenya, specialising in distributed systems, payment integrations, and polyglot microservices using Python (Django, FastAPI) and Go (Gin, Chi, Fiber).

Blockchain roots go back to internship-era Ethereum node deployments. Currently also building a production Sacco ERP with digital signatures and multi-team loan workflows across 8+ microservices.
